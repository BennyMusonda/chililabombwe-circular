THE CHALLENGE

Waste can be a problem. But it can also be an opportunity.

Recyclable materials are often thrown away because residents and businesses do not have a simple way to connect with collectors.

At the same time, collectors and recycling businesses need better ways to find, coordinate and track recyclable materials.

Our Solution

Chililabombwe Circular creates a digital connection between communities, collectors and the circular economy.

  • ✓ Report recyclable waste
  • ✓ Request collection
